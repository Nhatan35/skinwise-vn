# SkinWise VN — Product Vision

## 1. Product description

SkinWise VN is an AI skincare education and routine safety assistant for Vietnamese users. It helps users build minimalist skincare routines, understand cosmetic ingredients, detect potentially irritating routine combinations, track skin progress, and receive clear Vietnamese explanations.

SkinWise VN is not a medical diagnosis application. It does not diagnose skin diseases, prescribe medication, or guarantee treatment outcomes.

## 2. Target users

### Primary users

Beginner skincare users in Vietnam, especially students and young adults who:

- Buy skincare through online channels.
- Follow reviews from TikTok, Shopee, livestreams, or social media.
- Have limited skincare knowledge.
- Have a limited budget.
- Are confused by ingredients and product claims.
- Need a simple, safe, consistent routine.

### Secondary users

- Intermediate skincare users who want to review their routine.
- Users with sensitive skin who want to avoid overusing active ingredients.
- Users who want to track routine consistency and skin changes.

## 3. Problem statement

Vietnamese skincare consumers increasingly buy beauty and personal care products online, but many users do not have enough product literacy to decide whether a product fits their skin profile, routine, and budget. They may overuse active ingredients, follow viral trends, skip sunscreen, change products too quickly, or fail to track product effects over time.

## 4. Value proposition

SkinWise VN helps users:

- Build safer and simpler routines.
- Understand cosmetic ingredients in beginner-friendly Vietnamese.
- Detect routine risks before irritation happens.
- Track routine consistency and skin observations.
- Make better product decisions without pressure or exaggerated promises.

## 5. Differentiation

SkinWise VN is differentiated by:

- Vietnamese-first explanations.
- Beginner-friendly routine safety.
- Budget-aware skincare planning.
- Rule-based safety engine before AI.
- No beauty scoring or appearance pressure.
- No medical diagnosis or treatment guarantees.
- Modular architecture designed for long-term scalability.

## 6. Non-goals

SkinWise VN will not:

- Diagnose skin diseases.
- Prescribe medicine.
- Guarantee acne, dark spot, or scar treatment.
- Replace dermatologists.
- Score user attractiveness.
- Encourage unrealistic appearance ideals.
- Recommend aggressive routines for beginners.
- Sell products as the main MVP goal.

## 7. Product principles

1. Safety before personalization.
2. Education before recommendation.
3. Minimalism before complexity.
4. User privacy before analytics.
5. Rule-based checks before AI explanations.
6. Transparency before conversion.
7. Vietnamese context before global generic advice.

## 8. Product boundaries

SkinWise VN can say:

- "This routine may be too active-heavy for a beginner."
- "This ingredient is commonly used for oiliness or acne-prone skin."
- "Consider simplifying your routine and monitoring your skin response."

SkinWise VN must not say:

- "You have a disease."
- "This will cure your acne."
- "This product guarantees results."
- "You do not need a dermatologist."
